The Power Of Educattion

Not any scripted cipher or silicon made
Can kindle lights of consciousness engaged
Beyond the shine of any lit display
The path of human thought is found in learning how to find the way

A soul, by grappling with an idea
Is formed, not found by lightning-quick reply
But knowledge deep, which is not to be bought cheap,
Can shape a mind of truth with lasting power to leap

An algorithm cannot grasp and read
What hearts can know, and willfully deny
True education kindles burning gold
Within the soul, and fuels the mind to fly

So let us keep that precious inner light
And keep our human power of the mind
Because we need to find our own direction
To build a world beyond a machine's design
